Privacy Policy

Effective 15 September 2026

Who uses this tool

Clip Reviewer is used only by its owner to publish their own video clips to their own accounts. It has no public sign-up and no other users.

Information the tool accesses

  • TikTok account information: the basic profile information TikTok provides when the owner signs in (such as the account's open ID), used only to connect the owner's account.
  • Upload permission: permission to upload videos as drafts to the owner's TikTok inbox.
  • Video files and post text that the owner creates and approves in the tool.

How the information is stored

Access tokens and account identifiers are stored only in a private configuration file on the owner's computer. They are not uploaded to any server operated by the tool, and they are used only to upload the owner's own videos.

Sharing

The tool does not sell, rent or share any information with third parties. Videos are sent only to the platforms the owner has connected (TikTok, YouTube, Instagram), under those platforms' own terms and privacy policies.

This website

This website has no cookies, trackers, analytics or forms.

Removing access

The owner can remove the tool's access at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions, and by deleting the stored tokens from their computer.

Changes

Any change to this policy is published on this page with a new effective date.
