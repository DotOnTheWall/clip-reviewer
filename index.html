Clip Reviewer

Clip Reviewer is a private publishing tool used by the creator behind the Elisha Long Clips channels. It edits short vertical clips on the creator's own computer and, after the creator approves a clip, sends it to the creator's own social media accounts.

What it does with TikTok

  • The creator signs in with TikTok and allows the tool to upload videos.
  • An approved clip is uploaded as a draft to the creator's TikTok inbox.
  • The creator opens the draft in the TikTok app, writes the caption, chooses who can see it and posts it.

The tool never posts on anyone else's behalf and is not offered to other users.
